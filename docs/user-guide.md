# Platform user guide

This quick guide covers the rider and organizer workflows that connect the two apps. For screen-by-screen instructions, see the [Rider app guide](https://github.com/kvijay77-in/tour-rider-app/blob/main/docs/user-guide/README.md) and the [Management app guide](https://github.com/kvijay77-in/tour-mgmt-app/blob/main/docs/user-guide/README.md).

## Rider

### Check-in status and self check-in

Open a ride from **Tour** to see its details and check-in history. When a registered ride has check-in records, choose **Ride info** or **Check-In**. If records are missing or not yet available, the ride opens to Ride info and the check-in status message explains that there are no records to show. Unregistered rides open to Ride info. A stage without a record has not been confirmed as reached. Rider-submitted records are labelled **Self**.

For today's ride, Home and **Check-In** show self check-in actions only when you are enrolled on the active tour and the live ride and organizer settings allow them. You need a connection. Normal stage check-ins are available while the ride is open or started; a Pit Stop must also be open. **Not Riding** is a Start-only action while the ride is open and before a Start check-in. **Quit Riding** is available only after the ride starts and before a Finish check-in. See the [Rider app feature guide](features/rider-app.md#my-check-ins) for the policy defaults and cancellation rules.

!!! danger "Quit Riding and rider safety"
    **Organisers' advice:** Wherever possible, quit at a Pit Stop so the team can record it for you and arrange safe transport for you and your bike. If you must quit on the road, contact the organisers immediately after confirming.

    **This cannot be undone from the app.** Only the organisers can cancel a Quit Riding check-in, after making sure you are safe to continue.

### Event registration and activities

Open **Events** to see events and their scheduled occurrences. When an event requires registration, register for each occurrence while it is planned; registration closes when it starts. When registration is not required, eligible riders participate without registering. During an active occurrence, follow the activity progress and use the actions offered for you. Depending on the activity, these can include marking it complete, choosing a required option, opting out, or cancelling an eligible record and repeating the activity after progress refreshes.

### Tour and ride documents

On a selected Tour or **Ride Details** screen, open **Documents** when the section is shown. Choose a file to download it and open it with an available viewer. Documents need a connection; they are saved in the app's private storage, not public device storage.

## Organizer

### Ride check-ins and corrections

Use the management app's **Ride Day** dashboard to follow rider progress. Rider-submitted check-ins are labelled **Self**. Use the **Log** to review and correct records when a cancellation is available; detailed steps and role limits are in the [Management app guide](https://github.com/kvijay77-in/tour-mgmt-app/blob/main/docs/user-guide/README.md).

**Quit Riding safety:** Only staff can cancel a Quit Riding check-in. Before confirming its cancellation, make sure the rider is safe and fit to resume. The rider app cannot cancel this record.

### Event registration and activities

Set whether registration is required in the event definition. Registration applies separately to each occurrence and can be changed only while that occurrence is planned. When registration is required, organizers can register or unregister a rider for that occurrence; unregistered riders cannot have activities recorded. When it is not required, all eligible tour riders participate. Rider self-service for an activity follows the event, activity, occurrence and occurrence-specific activity settings, with the most specific setting taking precedence. Baggage custody and reconciliation remain staff work.

### Tour and ride documents

Administrators manage tour files and ride-specific files in **Artifacts**. Riders see a Documents section on the relevant Tour or Ride Details screen when files are available. Downloads need a connection and open from the rider app's private storage.
